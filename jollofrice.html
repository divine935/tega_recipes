BELOW ARE SOME OF THE INGREDIENTS NEEDED TO MAKE YOUR SMOKY NIGERAIN JOLLOF RICE:

  1. Tomatoes and Pepper.
  2. Scotch Bonnet, Green Pepper and Red Pepper
  3. Groundnut Oil
  4. Rice
  5. Onions
  6. Thyme, Curry, and any other spices of your choice.

BELOW ARE THE STEPS TAKEN TO MAKE YOUR JOLLOF RICE

  • Blend the pepper mix 🌶️ Blend tomatoes, red bell peppers, Scotch bonnet peppers, and onions until smooth.
  • Fry the stew base 🍅 Heat oil in a pot, add chopped onions, then pour in the blended pepper. Cook until the mixture thickens and the excess water reduces.
  • Add seasoning and stock 🧂 Add seasoning cubes, curry powder, thyme, salt, and chicken/beef stock. Let it simmer for about 10–15 minutes.
  • Add the rice 🍚 Wash your rice and add it to the stew. Stir well, cover the pot tightly, and cook on low heat until the rice absorbs the sauce and becomes tender.
  • Steam and serve 😋 Once the rice is cooked, fluff it gently and let it steam for a few minutes. Serve with fried plantain, chicken, or your favorite side.
