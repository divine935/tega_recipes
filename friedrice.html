FRIED RICE is one meal that has been eaten at least once by every Nigerian because of how delicious it is.

That's why it had to be among our list of top recipes.

Cooking is the most enjoyable and fun thing to do because not only does it satisfy you, it nourishes you as well.

BELOW ARE SOME OF THE INGREDIENTS NEEDED TO MAKE YOUR FRIED RICE:

  1. Rice.
  2. Green, Yellow and Red Pepper
  3. Groundnut Oil
  4. Protein
  5. Thyme, Curry, and any other spices of your choice.

BELOW ARE THE STEPS TAKEN TO MAKE YOUR FRIED RICE

  • Cook the protein 🍗 Season chicken, beef, or shrimp and cook until tender. Keep the stock for later.
  • Prepare the vegetables 🥕 Chop carrots, green beans, sweet corn, peas, and spring onions into small pieces.
  • Fry the vegetables 🥦 Heat some oil in a large pan, add the vegetables, and stir-fry for a few minutes. Add seasoning, curry powder, and a little salt.
  • Add the cooked rice 🍚 Add cooked, cooled rice to the vegetables. Stir everything together and add a little chicken stock for flavor.
  • Stir-fry and serve 😋 Keep stirring over medium heat until the rice is well mixed and slightly fried. Add your cooked chicken or shrimp, then serve hot.
